Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Side Quest Spanish ("we") uses a private tool to publish its own videos to its own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts through the platforms' official APIs.

What data we access

Only the data of our own accounts that the platforms return when we publish: basic account information (such as the account ID and name) and the status of the videos we upload. We do not access, collect or store data about other users or viewers.

How we use it

Solely to upload and publish our own videos and check that publishing succeeded. Access tokens are stored encrypted, only for our own publishing tool, and are never shared or sold. On YouTube the tool uploads our videos, sets their schedule and thumbnail, and posts comments on our own videos.

Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties.

Deletion

We can revoke the tool's access at any time from the platforms' settings; stored tokens are then deleted.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions.

Contact

Message us on Instagram or through our YouTube channel.
