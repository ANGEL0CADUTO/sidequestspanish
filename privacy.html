Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Side Quest Spanish ("we") uses a private tool to publish its own videos to its own TikTok and Instagram accounts through the platforms' official APIs.

What data we access

Only the data of our own accounts that the platforms return when we publish: basic account information (such as the account ID and name) and the status of the videos we upload. We do not access, collect or store data about other users or viewers.

How we use it

Solely to upload and publish our own videos and check that publishing succeeded. Access tokens are stored only on our own computer and are never shared or sold.

Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties.

Deletion

We can revoke the tool's access at any time from the platforms' settings; stored tokens are then deleted.
